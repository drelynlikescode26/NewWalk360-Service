Residential Housing & Independent Living

Temporary and transitional housing, residential options, and practical transition support for veterans, adults with disabilities, families, and seniors.

Who we focus on: Veterans, adults with disabilities, families, and seniors. Housing inquiries are welcome from people of all ages and genders.

  • Temporary housing and eligible hotel assistance
  • Transitional housing and a next-step housing plan
  • Group homes and shared housing
  • Independent living housing
  • Nonmedical, disability-inclusive housing
  • Eligible move-in assistance and housing resource connections
