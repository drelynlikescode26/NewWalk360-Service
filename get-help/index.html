COMMON QUESTIONS

Before You
Reach Out.

Are these services available now?

Check the availability label for each program. Unconfirmed or planned programs are not open applications. Independent providers in the resource directory may offer other options.

Is financial assistance guaranteed?

No. Rent, utility, hotel, and eligible prescription-cost assistance depend on program eligibility and available funding.

Does New Walk 360 offer mental health treatment?

Mental health support on this site means resources and referrals. Contact the provider for details about its services. Contact the listed provider for its services.

Can I upload documents here?

No personal documents are collected on this website. A confirmed secure intake service must be connected before applications or referrals open.

What if I need help right now?

Call 211 for local community referrals. Call or text 988 for emotional crisis support. For immediate danger, call 911.
