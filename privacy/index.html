Privacy &
accessibility.

Straightforward information about using this website.

Your information

This website does not currently accept housing applications, payment details, or uploaded personal documents. Resource searches and donation amount selections run in your browser and are not submitted to New Walk 360.

Website hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting providers may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to deliver the website. Please see GitHub’s privacy statement (opens in a new tab) for its practices.

External resources and giving

Provider links open third-party websites. Their privacy policies, service areas, eligibility rules, and intake procedures apply. If an external donation service is linked, payment is handled by that service; this website does not collect card details.

Photography & credits

This website uses licensed stock photography for illustration. People shown are models, not actual New Walk 360 clients, volunteers, or staff, and the photographs do not document our programs or imply endorsement. The organization’s supplied purple and gold logo is used as provided.

Photography from Pexels: Barbara Olsen — family moment; MART PRODUCTION — moving home; Ron Lach — conversation; Gustavo Fring — volunteers. Used under the Pexels License.

Accessibility

This website supports keyboard navigation, visible focus indicators, descriptive image text, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion preferences. Resource links and housing information are available without JavaScript. Interactive search and amount selections require JavaScript.

If you encounter difficulty, use the contact page for the organization’s current communication options. For community resource referrals by phone, call 211.
