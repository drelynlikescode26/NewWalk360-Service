ABOUT NEW WALK 360

Stability. Independence.
Community.

A comprehensive approach to housing, everyday needs, and the support that helps people move forward.

OUR MISSION

A Foundation
For What’s Next.

To empower individuals and families toward stability and independence by providing access to safe housing, essential resources, emergency assistance, and supportive services that strengthen lives and communities.

OUR VISION

Stronger Lives.
Stronger Communities.

To build communities where every individual and family has access to safe housing, essential resources, and opportunities to achieve lasting stability, independence, and self-sufficiency.

OUR FOCUS

Housing And The Support
Around It.

A stable home matters. So do food, clothing, healthcare connections, and help navigating difficult moments.

Our housing focus includes veterans, adults with disabilities, families, and seniors, with inquiries welcome from people of all ages and genders. Temporary stays, transitional housing, and longer-term residential options are explained alongside their proposed support and current availability.
