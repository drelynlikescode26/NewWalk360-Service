BEFORE YOU GET STARTED

A Few Helpful
Things To Know.

Who is this service for?

Nonprofit organizations looking for support with grant readiness, funding research, proposal development, or application preparation. The team will confirm whether the requested support is a fit.

Does consulting guarantee grant funding?

No. Funders make their own eligibility and award decisions. Consulting support does not guarantee an award or a particular funding amount.

What should we prepare for an inquiry?

A brief overview of your organization, the project you want to fund, your goals, and any known deadline is a useful starting point. The team can confirm what additional materials are needed.

How much does it cost, and when can we start?

Availability, pricing, and timelines are confirmed directly with the team based on the agreed scope. No consultation booking or payment is collected on this website.
