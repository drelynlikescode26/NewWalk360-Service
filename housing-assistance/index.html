BEFORE YOU REACH OUT

A little preparation
can make things easier.

Depending on the program, a provider may ask about your location, household, income, or current living situation. Requirements differ. You can ask for help even if you don’t have every document ready.

  • Write down your main questions and any urgent deadlines.
  • Ask whether the program serves your city or county.
  • Confirm how and where to submit any requested documents.
  • Keep personal documents private until you’re connected with the provider.
Does New Walk 360 provide emergency shelter?

This website does not confirm an emergency shelter or live bed availability. For local options, call 211 or search HUD’s shelter finder. Contact providers directly to confirm availability.

Can I apply for rent assistance here?

This site does not currently accept financial assistance applications. Use the directory to find providers and ask about their intake process, funding, and eligibility requirements.

Do I need to share personal documents on this website?

No. We do not ask you to upload identity, financial, or housing documents here. A provider can explain its own secure intake process when you contact it.
