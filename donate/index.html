GIVING WITH CLARITY

Your questions.
Clear answers.

Can I make a donation on this website today?

Online payments are not yet connected. The amount selector is a planning tool, and no gift is charged or submitted. Confirm a giving method directly with the organization before sending money.

Can I set up a recurring gift?

The monthly option helps you plan a recurring amount. A recurring gift is only established if you complete that setup through an active donation provider.

Will I receive a tax receipt?

Before giving, ask the organization to confirm its legal name, current tax status, and donation receipt process. Tax treatment depends on the gift and the organization’s status.
