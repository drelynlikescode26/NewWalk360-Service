# Transparent website logo assets

Prepared October 29, 2026 in response to the owner requesting removal of the logo's block-like appearance.

The original supplied image remains intact in images/logo.jpg. Built-in image_gen edits produced two PNG assets with alpha transparency: images/logo-transparent-light.png for dark backgrounds and images/logo-transparent-purple.png for light backgrounds. These are derived logo adaptations, not pixel-identical extractions. The nonprofit name, gold 360 and orbital-arrow motif remain recognizable. Both files were inspected and their alpha channels verified before integration.

## Extraction prompt (built-in image_gen)

Use case: background-extraction. Asset type: transparent nonprofit website logo. Input image 1 is the edit target, the supplied New Walk 360 logo. Extract ONLY the central logo artwork: the white italic NEW WALK lettering, the large gold 360, the gold elliptical orbital arrows around 360, and the smaller gold INC below. Preserve the exact spelling NEW WALK, 360, INC, typography, proportions, angle, white and gold colors, gold shading, and central arrangement as closely as possible. Remove the entire purple rectangular background, dots, corner decorative gold/purple swooshes, detached decorative top line and glows. The result must be a clean freestanding logo with real alpha transparency, no rectangular plate, no purple backdrop, no invented badge shape, no checkerboard drawn into the pixels. Keep crisp smooth edges and only subtle edge shading needed to read the white text. Center the extracted logo on a tightly fitted landscape transparent canvas, a small safe margin around all artwork, with no clipped letters or arrows. This is background removal from the existing logo, not a new brand design.
